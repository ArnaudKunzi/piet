MinGW-w64 winpthreads (https://sourceforge.net/projects/mingw-w64/)
Statically linked into cobra.exe and piet_native.exe via the "posix"
threading variant of gcc-mingw-w64 (backs <pthread.h> / std::thread /
Threads::Threads). Windows build only — macOS uses the platform's native
pthread implementation, not this one; this notice does not apply there.

Toolchain: mingw-w64-x86-64-dev 10.0.0-3 (Debian 12).
